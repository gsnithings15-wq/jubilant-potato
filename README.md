# Neo AI

Your personal AI assistant. Chat in English or தமிழ், with voice input and read-aloud.

## Features

- Chat with AI in English and Tamil
- Voice input (tap the mic and speak)
- Listen to replies out loud
- Copy any reply
- Saved chat history
- Light and dark mode
- Installable on your phone (PWA)

## Files

- `index.html`: the app
- `manifest.json`: lets you install it on your home screen
- `sw.js`: service worker for faster loading

## Setup

1. Open `index.html`.
2. Replace `PASTE_YOUR_CLOUDFLARE_WORKER_URL_HERE` with your Cloudflare Worker URL.
3. Go to Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, then main and / (root).
4. Open `https://neo-ai.github.io/neo-ai/`.

## Install on your phone

Open the link in Chrome, then tap **Add to Home screen**.
